About SafeHer

SafeHer is an AI-powered women safety system that leverages machine learning to detect unsafe situations based on location, time, and environmental data. Our mission is to provide women with a reliable safety companion that automatically alerts guardians and authorities during emergencies, ensuring faster response times and better protection.
